Conservation Tips

🔧
Fix Leaky Faucets
A single leaky faucet can waste up to 34 liters per day. Fixing it is the easiest water-saving win.
🚿
Shorter Showers
Cut your shower by 2 minutes and save up to 40 liters. Set a timer to stay on track.
🧺
Full Loads Only
Run dishwashers and washing machines only with full loads to maximize water efficiency.
🌧️
Rainwater Harvesting
Collect rainwater for garden use. A simple barrel can capture thousands of liters per year.
