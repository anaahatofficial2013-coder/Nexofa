About EcoSphere

We're building the platform that makes environmental action accessible, measurable, and rewarding for everyone.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

Two beliefs that shape every feature we build and every decision we make.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower individuals, students, and communities with the tools, knowledge, and motivation they need to understand their environmental footprint and take meaningful action — turning awareness into measurable impact every single day.

🔭

Our Vision

A world where every person understands their environmental impact, where sustainability is a shared habit — not a privilege, and where data-driven decisions drive a collective movement toward a healthier, greener planet for future generations.

The Urgency

Why Sustainability Matters

The environmental crisis isn't a distant threat — it's here, and the numbers speak for themselves.

🌡️
+1.2 °C
Global Temperature Rise

Average global temperature has already risen 1.2 °C above pre-industrial levels, accelerating extreme weather events worldwide.

🧴
11M Tons
Plastic Entering Oceans

Over 11 million metric tons of plastic waste flow into our oceans every year, devastating marine ecosystems and entering our food chain.

💧
2.2B People
Lack Safe Water Access

Nearly two-thirds of the world's population experiences severe water scarcity for at least one month per year, a figure projected to worsen.

🌳
10M Hectares
Forest Lost Annually

We lose roughly 10 million hectares of forest each year — an area the size of Iceland — destroying biodiversity and carbon sinks.

Our Journey

EcoSphere Through the Years

From a small idea to a global movement — here's how we got here.

2024

Founded

EcoSphere was born out of a university hackathon — a shared frustration that no simple tool existed for students to track and reduce their personal carbon footprint.

2025

Beta Launch

After months of research and development, we launched our closed beta to 500 university students, introducing the carbon calculator, challenges, and gamified rewards.

2025

10,000 Users

Word spread quickly. Within six months of beta, we crossed 10,000 active users across 15 countries, proving the appetite for accessible sustainability tools.

2026

Global Expansion

EcoSphere officially launches worldwide — partnering with universities, NGOs, and corporations to bring intelligent environmental tracking to communities everywhere.
